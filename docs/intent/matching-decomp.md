# Intent: Companions of Xanth matching decomp

Confirmed 2026-09-07.

- Outcome: Byte-identical matching decomp of *Companions of Xanth* from this
  1997 budget CD, Super Mario 64 style, same practice as Diablo / Musashi /
  Xenogears.
- User: Continuing that decomp work.
- Why now: The disc is here and the project is starting.
- Success: `XANTH.EXE` + `XANTH.OVL` from this disc are the authority. First
  win is register the ISO, pin hashes, identify the original compiler, and
  stand up a matching pipeline. Done means a rebuild that diffs empty against
  those two files. A modern port/remaster is derived from that decomp later,
  not a replacement engine.
- Constraint: 16-bit DOS overlay game. No game data or proprietary toolchain
  in git. Retail bytes win over “cleaner” C.
- Out of scope: Reimplementation-first, a different SKU, art remaster, and
  modernizing in the matching tree.
